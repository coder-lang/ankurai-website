How HireIQ AI Interview Agent Works

  1. Candidate gets link via WhatsApp/Email — HireIQ sends personalized link in Hindi/English
  2. AI conducts 15-min structured interview — HireIQ asks role-specific questions (Backend Engineer, Sales, etc.)
  3. Transcript + audio + video captured — Every word, emotion, cheat detection
  4. LLM scores against your rubric — Technical 8.5/10, Communication 9/10, Problem Solving 8/10
  5. Report pushed to ATS / Google Sheet / Email automatically — No manual entry
  6. You interview only top 20% — 70% reduction in screening time
