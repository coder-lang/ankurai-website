What is Agentic AI? Why Now?

Agentic AI = AI agents that use tools, have memory, hand off to other agents, and run end-to-end workflows without human re-reading. Example: Intake → Verification → Scoring → Routing → Follow-up — all autonomous. LangGraph is the framework to build this.
