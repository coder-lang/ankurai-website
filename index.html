How HireIQ Works — Built by IIT Roorkee AI Engineer

  1. Candidate gets interview link via WhatsApp/Email from HireIQ
  2. AI conducts 15-min structured interview — HireIQ asks role-specific questions
  3. Transcript + audio + video captured by HireIQ
  4. LLM scores candidate against your rubric (Technical, Communication, Problem Solving)
  5. Report pushed to your ATS / Google Sheet / Email automatically
  6. You interview only top 20% — 70% faster hiring
