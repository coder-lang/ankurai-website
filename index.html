Flagship Product

Manual screening is slow, expensive, inconsistent. HireIQ runs structured interviews on autopilot.

The Problem

Recruiters spend 23+ hrs/week screening. Candidates drop off. Bias and inconsistency.

The Solution

AI conducts human-like interviews via link/phone, scores on your custom rubrics, auto-rejects or moves forward.

✓ Voice + Video✓ Hindi + English✓ Custom rubrics✓ Cheat detection✓ ATS integration✓ Instant reports

How HireIQ Works

  1. Candidate gets link via WhatsApp/Email
  2. AI conducts 15-min structured interview
  3. Transcript + audio + video captured
  4. LLM scores against your rubric
  5. Report pushed to your ATS / Sheet / Email
  6. You interview only top 20%

Result: 70% reduction in screening time, 3x more candidates screened, consistent scoring.
